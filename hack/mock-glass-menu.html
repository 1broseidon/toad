The glass menu, three ways

All three fix the same three things: every item gets a label (an unlabeled monitor glyph was doing the confusing work), the word becomes Desktop — "computer" belongs to the agents' toad-computers — and adding a teammate stops being a billboard. Same Liquid Glass plugin, different geometry. The under-glow and lens ideas are borrowed from your reference; the shapes are ours.

A

One object — the labeled trio

The top-right pill is gone; one piece of chrome total. Desktop lives in the bar with a live status dot on its icon (moss = wired). The add is a compact moss disc — present, not shouting. Tap Desktop → the Desktops sheet.

B

Two layers — the desktop on the shoulder

Your reference's real move: a small secondary pill riding the main deck. The named desktop becomes that pill — status, name, and switcher in one — and the top-right pill is gone. The deck below holds two labeled acts.

C

The lens — add is the raised act

The reference's raised circle, spent on the one act that makes something: a glass lens, moss glyph, faint under-glow — the only glow in the app. Desktop and Settings sit labeled beside it. Top-right pill gone here too.

Common to all three

  1. "Desktop", never "computers". The half-sheet retitles to Desktops; "computer" is reserved for the agents' toad-computers everywhere a person reads.
  2. Every flat item is labeled — 11 px Space Grotesk under a 22 pt glyph. The unlabeled gear survives nowhere.
  3. A and C keep a status dot on the Desktop glyph (moss = wired, hollow = looking) so killing the pill loses no information. B keeps the name itself visible, which is the strongest status of all.
  4. My read: B if you want the name always on screen, A if you want the quietest possible screen. C is the best-looking and the least conventional.
