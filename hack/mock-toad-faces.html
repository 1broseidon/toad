The toads, redrawn

The mark is two eyes on a body with the pupils punched through — one shape, one colour, nothing painted on top. The current faces betray that: squashed eyeball ellipses, painted pupils, parts assembled instead of one creature. This pass rebuilds every face with the mark's exact construction, then parameterizes it into trait slots — bodies, gazes, mouths, hats, markings, patterns, seven hues — the way a generative collection is built, minus the gimmicks. Same Face type, same stored configs, zero migration: only the drawing changes.

The mark, for reference: body rx 6, full-circle eye domes centred on its top edge, slit pupils punched by mask. Every face below is this drawing, varied.

Before / after — the four on the roster

Same stored configs, both renderers. The new eyes are always full domes — expression moved into the pupil aperture and punched lids, which is where the mark keeps it. Every toad gains feet: two small nubs that ground the silhouette as one creature.

02

The construction

Layered like a generative collection, drawn like a logo. Every layer is either ink or a punch through the ink — never a second colour on top.

03

The traits

One slot varies per row; everything else holds. This is the whole vocabulary — no lasers, no sunglasses; accessories stay things a toad in this app might actually wear.

04

The collection

Forty-eight deterministic draws at roster size, trait line under each — the sheet a collection generator would print. The test: any two neighbours should read as different people from the same family.

The same eight at rail size (30 px): fine punches drop, mouths and pupils gain weight — the mark's own small-size concession.

05

What ships

  1. FaceIcon v2 replaces the drawing in FaceIcon.tsx. The Face type is untouched — every stored teammate re-renders in the new geometry the moment the build lands. The agent's compose flow and Shuffle keep working unchanged.
  2. Punched, not painted: pupils, lids, mouths, body markings and the beanie's rib are all mask punches — on a waterline face the pupil slit genuinely shows the deep water colour through the eye, same trick as the logo sitting in its waterline.
  3. Feet are unconditional — they are the new silhouette signature. If you want a variant without them, say so and they become a trait.
  4. Optional, flag if wanted: one new pattern (speckle) at the end of the pattern row — an additive enum value, old faces unaffected.
