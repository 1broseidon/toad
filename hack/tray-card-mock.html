Two states, because there are only two things to say

Left is what you get when something is running: one row per busy teammate, each with its own live glyph — the same component the composer uses, so a teammate reaching for a tool ratchets and one writing a reply shows dots. That is the part a menu cannot do at any price.

Right is everything else. No rows, no "0 active", no chart of nothing — one line telling you it is quiet, and the two things you opened this for. The card gets smaller when there is less to say.
