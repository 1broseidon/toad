Toad — the eyes have it

One idea, four proportions. The tell is that a toad's eyes sit on top of the head and break its outline — so the crown bulges twice, and the pupils are punched straight through. Still one flat silhouette, still no cartoon.

A · Squat

Wide and low. Most toad, least logo — nearly a landscape.

B · Balanced

Eyes further apart and higher, body squared to a soft base.

C · Compact

Tighter and taller, closer to a square lockup for an app icon.

D · Waterline

Same head, cut off flat by the surface it is sitting in.
