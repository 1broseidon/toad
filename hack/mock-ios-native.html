Toad iOS — the native pass

Refined and narrowed to what's decided: the glass action bar at the foot of Team, the computer pill at its head — both rendered by a native SwiftUI layer with real Liquid Glass — and settings as pushed screens in our own grammar. Faces below are drawn by the app's actual face renderer; every colour is a project token. iPhone 16 Pro canvas, true metrics.

01

Team

The web footer stack is gone. Three floating things remain: the roster owns the screen, the pill says which desktop this team lives on, the bar holds the only three actions the screen has. Rows sink into a fade before they pass under the glass, the same way the transcript settles above the composer.

02

The material — Swift, not CSS

The bar and the pill are not DOM. A single native layer floats over the WebView and draws both with the system's own glass, so the roster genuinely lenses through them as it scrolls. Everything else on screen stays React.

Host
A FloatingChrome Capacitor plugin: one SwiftUI overlay above the WKWebView holding the bar and the pill. Touches land on the chrome; everywhere else they pass through to the page.
Material
iOS 26: glassEffect(.regular, in: .capsule) — real Liquid Glass, lensing edge and all. iOS 17–25: .ultraThinMaterial capsule with a hairline. Android & web mode: the CSS recipe in this page, same geometry.
Icons
SF Symbols on device — desktopcomputer, plus, gearshape — 17 pt semibold, so they render like every other iOS app the thumb knows.
Accent
The Teammate capsule stays solid moss on every platform. Glass is the shell; the one saturated thing on screen is still the primary act.
Bridge
JS drives it: chrome.set({computer, linked, working}) updates the pill; taps come back as chrome:action. The layer hides itself when a conversation is pushed or the keyboard rises.
03

Settings

Approved shape, tightened metrics: 30 pt icon tiles, 54 pt rows, half-point hairlines that start after the icon column, caps labels in Space Grotesk, values whispered on the right. Tools, Workspace, and MCP stay on the desktop; one quiet line says so.

04

New teammate

A sheet, not a form. Face first — shuffle until you like them — then a name, an optional brief, and the harness. The button names the person it's about to create.

05

Still open
