Three glyphs that know what a toad is

The working indicator is currently three marks that orbit and then fall into a row. It reads as a spinner because that is what it is — the motion could belong to any app. None of it says toad.

Each candidate keeps the rule that makes the current one work: the same three marks the whole time, changing formation rather than being swapped out, so the typing dots are where the glyph went instead of what replaced it. What changes is the vocabulary of the movement — breath, a hop, a thing watching from the water. Judge them at true size first; the ×8 stage is for seeing what the motion is, not for deciding.

Stage border is the exact 28×16 viewBox at ×8 — a mark crossing it is a mark leaving the box. The ×4 strip runs all three formations at once and ignores the phase control, so the formations can be compared without waiting on a morph. The bottom row is one cycle of the selected phase frozen into six stills — a live loop tells you a thing moves, six stills tell you the path it takes and where the time goes.

Every candidate is the same architecture as the shipping component: poseOf(phase, index, t) per mark, a 300ms eased blend between the formation being left and the one being entered, one requestAnimationFrame loop. Poses here carry separate sx/sy so a mark can squash on landing, which the current single scale cannot express.

No external dependencies. Type is the system stack, not Space Grotesk / Geist.
