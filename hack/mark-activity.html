The vocabulary

Stillness is the loudest thing here. Everything moves except blocked, which stops dead and looks straight at you. A permission request is the one moment the agent genuinely cannot continue without you, and it is currently just another bubble to scroll past.

A stall slows it down rather than speeding it up. Past six seconds in one tool the motion drags, because "working hard" would be a lie about a process that is stuck. Watch bun test around 0:26.
