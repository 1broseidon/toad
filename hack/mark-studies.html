Toad — mark studies

Flat, geometric, no cartoon. Each shown at 64 / 32 / 16px, and once on light. The tag says how well it survives becoming the three-mark working glyph.

1 · Waterline

Two eyes breaking a flat surface — the one toad silhouette that isn't a cartoon. Three separate shapes, so it is already the glyph standing still. The bar doubles as a message line.

morphs: direct
2 · Counter

The eyes cut out of the head rather than added on. One shape, two holes — the most logo-like of the set and the one that holds up smallest, because negative space survives where thin parts don't.

morphs: holes escape
3 · Triad

The working glyph at rest. Two eyes and a snout, read as a face only because of the spacing. Weakest as "toad" on its own, strongest as a system — the logo and the indicator are literally the same three marks.

morphs: it is the glyph
4 · Leap

Toad as motion instead of anatomy: the arc of a jump over a landing point. The most abstract and the most generic — this is the control, to show how far the others are actually reaching.

morphs: poorly
