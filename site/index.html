Local-first is not a feature flag.

Your conversations, your code, and your API keys stay on the hardware they already live on. Nothing routes through a server we run — there isn’t one. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, nothing collected.

Your phone pairs by pointing its camera at a QR code on your desktop, and talks to it over your own network. The phone appears in a list on your computer, and you can revoke it there any time.

The external agents you attach sign in themselves — Toad never holds their credentials, by design.
